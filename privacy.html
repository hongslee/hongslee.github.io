yhits radio: Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Who uses it. yhits radio is used only by its owner's household. No one else can sign in.

What it accesses. Read-only access to the owner's Google Calendar (upcoming events) and Gmail (new incoming messages). It never sends, changes or deletes email or calendar events.

How the data is used. Event titles, times and places are turned into short spoken reminders. New emails are summarised in one sentence to decide whether they need the owner's action; the summary may be spoken aloud at home. Email text is processed by the Anthropic Claude API for that purpose only.

Storage. A small log of message IDs, senders, subjects and summaries is kept on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google API Services. yhits radio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
